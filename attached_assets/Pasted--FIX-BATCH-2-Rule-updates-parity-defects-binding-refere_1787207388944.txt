## 1. SHEET LAW v2 (rule change — replaces the previous hero minimum)

- Detail page WITH at least one photo: on initial render the sheet occupies at
  most 2/3 of the viewport height — the hero always keeps at least 1/3
  (prototype: `.sc:has(.sheet) .dhero { min-height: 33.4% }`). Longer content
  scrolls as before; the sheet remains content-height when content is short.
- Detail page WITHOUT a photo: no cap — the content-height sheet may extend all
  the way to the top if the content needs it. The ambient-gradient hero (FIX 2
  of batch 1) still fills whatever space remains above.
- Modal bottom sheets (sign-in, legal and similar `v--sheet` types): max-height
  is now the full screen (prototype `max-height: 100%`), still bottom-anchored
  and content-height, inner scroll when taller.

Verify: computed hero height ≥ 33% of viewport on a photo detail (e.g. Skuter)
at 390×844, plus a screenshot of one long text-only page reaching high.

## 2. NO WEEKLY HOURS TABLES (rule change)

Remove the per-day "Odpiralni čas" table from ALL venue/POI detail pages. The
detail page shows ONLY the status chip: `odprto do HH:MM` (today's closing
time) when open, or `zaprto` when closed. Najbližje rows and any list rows show
`<distance> · odprto do HH:MM` or `<distance> · zaprto` — never day ranges,
never "Danes HH:MM–HH:MM". Cross-midnight open/closed computation stays exactly
as fixed in batch 1. The weekly data stays in the DB untouched — only the UI
stops rendering it.
