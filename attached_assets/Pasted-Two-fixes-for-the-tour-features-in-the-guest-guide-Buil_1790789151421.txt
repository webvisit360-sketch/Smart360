Two fixes for the tour features in the guest guide. Build in dev. Report and STOP. No publication.

1. RECORDING ENTRY POINT MOVES TO ITS OWN TAB
The "Posnemi svojo turo" card currently renders inside category listings (including "Vse"), which clutters the content. Change:
- Remove the card from ALL category listings entirely.
- Instead, when tour_recording_enabled is on, render a SYNTHETIC tab/chip "Snemanje tur" in the Okolica tab row, positioned immediately AFTER the category whose normalized label is "Kolesarjenje" (if absent, after "Pohodništvo"; if both absent, last). Selecting it shows the recorder view (activity choice, Začni snemanje, Moj profil, privacy note) as the tab's content.
- CRITICAL: this is a UI-level pseudo-tab only, rendered in the guest guide when the setting is on. It must NOT create a category/section in the content structure, must not appear in the admin structure, tab-ordering dialogs, readiness checks, or the host form, and must not enter the translation pipeline (its label is localized in code: SL "Snemanje tur", EN "Record a tour", DE "Tour aufzeichnen", IT "Registra un tour").
- Styling identical to real category chips/tabs; works in normal and fullscreen flows as before.

2. "MOJ PROFIL" CARD CONTRAST BUG
On the dark theme, the white "Moj profil" card renders its title and field labels (Starost, Spol, Teža, Vrsta kolesa) nearly invisible — they inherit light-on-dark text colors. Fix: inside the profile card, title and labels use the CGP on-white tokens (title #121A14, labels #66716A) regardless of the surrounding theme. Audit the tour summary and any other white-on-dark cards in the tour UI for the same inherited-color bug and fix consistently.
