The recording is measured, and it rules out both the clip hypothesis and the
conditional-render hypothesis. Your inventory was right: the grabber and the
radius are unconditional. The variable is the HERO HEIGHT.

Measured, first open, 60 fps, iPhone:
  1.30 - 1.45 s   panel top edge at y=347, square corners, no grabber visible
  1.467 s         panel top edge JUMPS UP to y=321 — exactly 26 px — and in the

  The title does not move. The panel grows upward by 26 px.

Those 26 px are the overlap band by which the panel covers the bottom of the
hero — the strip that carries the grabber and the corner radius. During the
slide that overlap is absent, so the panel sits 26 px lower and its top edge is
square because the rounded part is the part that is missing.

The cause is the hero's height, not the panel. While the hero image is not yet
decoded the hero renders at a provisional height; the panel is positioned
relative to it; when the real height lands, everything shifts. Your Chromium
runs never show it because the image is cached and decodes in a few ms. The
owner's phone on mobile data shows it every single time.
