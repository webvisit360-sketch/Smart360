=====================================================================
1. THE A6 LABEL — TENANT LOGO, NO SMART360
=====================================================================

The label hangs in a guest room. What means something there is the accommodation, not the
system the page runs on.

  <img class="pc__logo" src="{tenant.logoTransparent}" alt="">   <!-- was the wordmark -->
  <div class="pc__qr">…QR…</div>
  <div class="pc__n">{tenant.name}</div>
  <div class="pc__s">Skenirajte za vse o nastanitvi in okolici<br>
                     <i>Scan for everything about your stay</i></div>
  <div class="pc__u">{url without the scheme}</div>

  .pc__logo{width:auto;max-width:46mm;max-height:34mm;height:auto;display:block;
            margin:0 auto 5mm}

  max-width AND max-height, because tenant logos arrive in every proportion: a wide
  wordmark and a tall crest must both fit the same block without pushing the QR code down
  the page. Never set a fixed height.

  Use the TRANSPARENT file, not the square white avatar — the label prints on white and
  the square would show as a grey-edged box on some printers.

  Keep the address line. It is not branding; it is what a guest types when the camera will
  not focus.

  Tenant with no logo: print the card headed by the name. Do NOT fall back to the Smart360
  wordmark here.

Same rule in the share sheet: no Smart360 wordmark anywhere in it. The QR plate, the
address pill and the rows stay as they are.

Smart360 branding now lives in exactly two places: the admin UI and the landing page at
smart360.info. Nowhere in the guest app, the label, or the share sheet.

VERIFY
  a) Print to PDF: tenant logo on top, no wordmark, QR still fully on the page.
  b) Try a deliberately wide logo (1200x200) and a tall one (400x1200): both fit the same
     block and nothing below moves.
  c) Scan the printed PDF from paper — it resolves to smart360.info/<slug>.
  d) A tenant with no logo still prints a usable card.

=====================================================================
2. THE /g/ REDIRECT — TEST IT PUBLISHED, NOT IN DEV
=====================================================================

The redirect is done in the app, not as a real 301, so it depends on two things that only
show up on the published static host.

  a) SPA FALLBACK
     The host must serve index.html for /g/<slug>/... . If it returns its own 404, our
     JavaScript never loads and the redirect never runs. Fetch
     https://smart360.info/g/meli-pu and confirm the body is the app shell, not a host 404.

  b) NO HISTORY LOOP
     The redirect must replace the history entry, not push one:
       history.replaceState(null, "", target)      // not location.href = target
     Test by hand: open /g/meli-pu, land on /meli-pu, press Back — you must leave the site,
     not bounce back to /g/meli-pu. Apply the same to the alias canonicalisation.

Report back: the HTTP status and first bytes of the /g/meli-pu response, and whether Back
leaves the site.
