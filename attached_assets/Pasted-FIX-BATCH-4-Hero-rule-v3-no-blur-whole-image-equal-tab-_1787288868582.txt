FIX 2 — HERO RULE v3 (binding extension C5 — replaces the IMG-1 blur-fill rule)

The owner rejects the blur backdrop except where it is unavoidable. New rule
for every detail hero and gallery slide:





Compute the image's natural aspect (w/h). Hero height =

containerWidth / imageAspect, clamped to [176px, 66%] of the viewport
 height.



If the full-width height fits inside the clamp: render the WHOLE image

edge-to-edge — no cropping, no blur, no side bands. The sheet starts
 directly below the hero: landscape photo → short hero, sheet high;
 portrait photo → tall hero, sheet low. The former "sheet ≤ 2/3" cap no
 longer applies on photo pages; the sheet fills the remaining space
 (content top-aligned, inner scroll as needed).



ONLY when the image is so portrait that full-width rendering would exceed

the 66% cap: hero = cap, image object-fit: contain by height, centered,
 with the blurred same-image fill on the LEFT/RIGHT bands only. This is the
 single case where blur is allowed (the owner explicitly approved it on the
 BFT key-box photo; the Čoln portrait photo falls into the same case). The
 whole photograph must always remain visible.



Blur is removed everywhere else. A landscape image must never be cropped

left/right nor blurred.



Galleries: hero height adapts to the ACTIVE slide with a smooth ~200 ms

height transition; each slide follows rules 1–3. Dots and the back button
 stay in place.



No-photo pages keep the ambient hero and content-height sheet unchanged.



Grid/list thumbnails keep cover (unchanged).

Verify with screenshots at 390×844: Skuter (whole scooter, NO blur, short
hero, sheet high), Parkirišče (whole photo incl. asphalt, no blur), Čoln 3h
