Why this changes: the previous screen wrapped 18 category chips over seven rows, consuming half the viewport before a single place was visible. The new screen shows ONE scrolling row of groups and gives the rest of the screen to large, readable place cards.

Rules:

The mapping is DATA, not hardcoded: store a group per category so the host can reassign them later in the admin. Ship these as the defaults.
A group with no visible content is not rendered at all.
The first group with content is selected on open.
Localize the five group names in SL/EN/DE/IT.
Card content rules
Photo: the item's first image, cover with focal point. When the item has no photo, use the theme ambient gradient — never a blank or broken box.
Meta line: distance · CATEGORY. Distance is omitted entirely when distanceMeters is null (do not print "0.0 km" — the competitor does that and it looks broken). Format: 850 m below 1 km, 1,2 km at or above.
For venues with opening hours, the meta line may carry the status instead of the category: 1,2 km · odprto do 22:00 / zaprto · odpre se ob 12:00, using the rules already implemented.
Description: the item's own text, clamped to two lines — never cut mid-sentence with an ellipsis button.
Verification
Computed-style pairs (prototype vs live) for: header kicker and title, tab row padding and gap, a selected and an unselected tab (height, padding, radius, font, colours), card width and radius, photo aspect ratio, body padding, meta row, category label, title, description and its clamp. Plus screenshots at 390 × 844 of: the default group, a switched group, a card without a distance, and a card for a venue showing open/closed status.
