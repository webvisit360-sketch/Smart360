  Krajinski park Golte · Alpski vrt Golte · Frančiškanski samostan Nazarje ·
  Stolnica svetih Mohorja in Fortunata · Dvorec Novo Celje · Rimska nekropola

Every one carries a generic type PHRASE before the name, which OSM does not
store: krajinski park, alpski vrt, samostan, stolnica, dvorec, nekropola,
skakalni center, razgledni stolp.

This is slap at Rinka and občina at Rečica, at scale. Our table holds eleven
single words. Slovene has many more, and a large share of them are two or
three words long.

The proof is Golte: in Step B the bare name "Golte" resolved cleanly. In run
three "Krajinski park Golte" and "Alpski vrt Golte" both fail.
