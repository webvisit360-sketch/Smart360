PRIMARY HYPOTHESIS, test it first: on iOS Safari, `overflow: hidden` combined
with `border-radius` is not reliably clipped while an ANCESTOR is animating a
`transform`. Safari drops the clip for the duration. Our sheet is exactly that
shape: a transformed ancestor containing a radius + overflow:hidden panel.
Standard remedies, in order of preference:
  a) put the radius on the element that is itself transformed, not on a
     descendant of it;
  b) replace `overflow:hidden` clipping with `clip-path: inset(0 round 30px)`
     or a `mask-image`, which Safari composites correctly;
  c) promote the clipping element to its own layer for the duration.
