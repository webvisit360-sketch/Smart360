"Snežna jama na Raduhi" → "Snežna jama" worked, but the token we removed is
exactly the one that distinguishes this cave from every other Snežna jama in
the country. The only guard left was distance, and distance happened to

Do not remove the mechanism. Mark it:
· record on the proposal that it was confirmed by a shortened query, storing
  both the original query and the shortened one;
· show it in the confirmation queue as a distinct badge, with both strings

  individual human decision.

A weakened confirmation is acceptable. A weakened confirmation that looks
identical to a strong one is not.
