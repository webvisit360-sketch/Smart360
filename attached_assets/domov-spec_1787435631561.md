# DOMOV — exact design spec (checkpoint 2 rework)

Binding reference: `prototip-2030.html` (attached, view `#v-home`). Copy the
values; do not re-derive them. All numbers measured at **390 × 844**, theme
`dan`. Colours are theme tokens, shown here as their `dan` values — use the
tokens, never the literals, so the other three themes follow automatically.

## Structure, top to bottom

1. Hero photo
2. Sheet, rising 26px over the photo, filling the rest of the screen
3. Kicker (tenant name) + title
4. Greeting strip (only when the guest is signed in)
5. WiFi button, full width
6. Three buttons: Obvestila · Sporočila · Zemljevid
7. Section "Danes" + horizontal card strip
8. Quiet "Pomoč in nujni primeri" link
9. Bottom bar

There is no "Za vas" card block. It was removed — it only duplicated the
bottom-bar tabs.

## Behaviour

- Hero photo comes from a new per-tenant admin field. When empty: the
  tenant's first gallery photo; when there is none: the theme ambient
  gradient. Never a flat block, never a broken image.
- The sheet must fill the screen to the bottom bar — no half-empty page.
- The greeting strip renders only when the guest is signed in; when it is
  absent nothing else shifts.
- "Danes" content:
  - tenant WITH a programme → today's events, nearest first;
  - tenant WITHOUT a programme → ONLY from Kolesarjenje, Pohodništvo,
    Izleti, Kulturna dediščina, Naravna dediščina;
  - NEVER from Trgovine, Pekarne, Bencinske črpalke, Bankomati, Lekarne,
    Bolnišnica;
  - nothing available at all → the whole section is hidden, never an empty
    frame.
- Every card: category kicker, title, one line of useful detail
  (distance or duration).
- Search and 360 are the only controls on the hero; no other control may
  overlay the photo.
- Both "Shrani" and "Pozneje" on the sign-in sheet land on Domov — never
  back on the cover.
