THREE ACCEPTED SHAPES, ONE ALWAYS REJECTED

1 · Short link. https://maps.app.goo.gl/... carries no coordinates and no
name. Expand it server-side by following the HTTP redirect, then parse the
destination. Restrict expansion to Google Maps hosts, follow at most three
redirects, and refuse anything that lands off Google — the parser must not
become a general-purpose URL fetcher.

2 · Search form. The expanded destination may be
    /maps/search/46.311167,+14.909133?entry=tts...
Those are the pin's coordinates, in the path. They are valid and must be
used. Note the URL-encoded comma-space. This form carries NO place name, so
step 1 must ask for the name separately rather than leaving it blank.

3 · Place form. /maps/place/Hotel+A+plus/@.../!3d46.2680471!4d15.188617
Use !3d and !4d. The name is the path segment after /place/, URL-decoded.

Always rejected: @lat,lng,zoom on its own. It is the map viewport, roughly
two hundred metres off in the Hotel A plus example, and must never be used as
a fallback when the other markers are missing. A link that yields no usable
coordinate fails loudly and asks for a different link.
