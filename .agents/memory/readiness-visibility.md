name: Readiness guest visibility
description: Empty categories are a deliberate host choice, not missing content.
---

Readiness must apply guest visibility rules to draft content, not penalize deliberately empty or inactive categories. With no guest-visible categories, photo coverage passes; the separate content check is responsible for missing content. Keep administrative pending-work totals distinct from guest-scoped readiness.

**Why:** The owner corrected a readiness penalty caused by intentionally empty categories that guests cannot see.

**How to apply:** When extending readiness, audit ancestor visibility and item eligibility, not just the row's own active flag. Do not confuse guest visibility rules applied to the working draft with switching readiness to the older published snapshot.
