---
name: Guest-home truthfulness
description: Product rules for ratings, response-time claims, and absent host/category imagery on the guest home.
---

The guest home must omit ratings until at least one real review exists, and omit response-time claims until at least five real guest-to-host exchanges have been measured. One exchange begins with the first unanswered guest message and ends with the next host message in that thread; consecutive guest messages before that reply count as one exchange. Display the median and round the displayed unit up.

**Why:** Empty ratings and guessed response promises are presented to guests as facts. Repeated stock imagery also disguises missing host-provided assets.

**How to apply:** Render type/region instead of a zero-review rating. Before five measured exchanges, show a responder’s explicitly stored name if available, otherwise no second line. Never substitute accommodation logos or stock images for a host photograph; missing category photography must remain visibly missing.
