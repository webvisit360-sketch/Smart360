---
name: Creator place entry boundary
description: Owner-approved boundary for adding new okolica places versus house content.
---

Do not add a separate “missing place” entry point in Creator. Operators add world places from the shared content skeleton under Nastanitev, inside `explore` and `services` categories.

**Why:** The shared skeleton is already the canonical place where operators manage content; another Creator entry point would duplicate the workflow and confuse ownership.

**How to apply:** Operator-added okolica entries begin with verified place search or the manual pin fallback, without a Creator review step. Matching pending proposals are resolved silently; unmatched legacy proposals stay dormant. Host form submission instead creates ordinary draft entries directly, including coordinate-less entries for uncertain matches; see host-form-draft-authority.md. New `stay` and `offer` entries remain ordinary house items with the existing plain flow.

Normal content management must not surface proposal rows or send operators to a Creator queue. Structure alignment and distance recalculation belong on Struktura vsebine; the Creator page remains only an advanced destination.

**Why:** On 2026-09-24 the owner explicitly replaced the proposal-based operator workflow with direct draft management.

**How to apply:** Keep host-submission provenance and materialization status in Vsebina gostitelja, linking to real entries or their archive rather than proposals. Publication remains a separate operator action.

Distances for okolica entries are owned by routing/geocoding systems, never typed by an operator or generated by a model.

**Why:** Distance and duration must remain reproducible measurements rather than editorial content.

**How to apply:** Verify coordinates server-side and compute road distance, duration, and range before materialization. Do not expose an authored distance field when creating an okolica item.

Machine-owned distance continues through editing: an active materialization makes distance read-only and recomputable from its stored coordinates. Only coordinate-less legacy items retain manual distance editing.

**Why:** Allowing normal edits or stale clients to overwrite a verified route silently breaks the provenance of guest-facing distance and duration.

**How to apply:** Enforce ownership server-side as well as in the UI. A recompute must atomically refresh every distance projection and preserve Creator classifications that are not simple duration buckets.
