# DEV report — local tour calorie estimate (STOP; not published)

## Scope and privacy
Optional profile (age, female/male, kg; cycling bike and electric assist) is stored in versioned browser localStorage with guarded reads/writes. The first start offers Save or Skip; a small Profile control is available in planned-GPX and free recording. Missing weight means **no kcal** in live map/tiles, result, or PNG. When storage is blocked, a visible warning explains the profile is only in page memory. Each recording receives an immutable profile snapshot and accumulated kcal in its bounded local tour backup; later edits apply to the **next** tour, not old segments. Legacy saved tours lacking a snapshot show no estimate. Nothing in the profile or kcal is sent to an API, analytics, console or GPX; GPX serializer is unchanged. PNG is explicitly a local schematic, not a tile screenshot.

## Models and constants
All results are net *moving-segment estimates*, rounded to the nearest 10 kcal, explicitly marked approximate; **not validated personal physiology or medical measurements**. Accepted horizontal fixes only: no interpolated gap, manual pause, auto-pause, rejected jump or reconstructed/downsampled-track segment contributes. Streaming grade uses a rolling ≥20m accepted-fix elevation window (short fixes use the last valid slope, flat until the first window). Grade resets to flat for missing/low-quality altitude (horizontal accuracy >15m or vertical accuracy >10m), vertical rate >2m/s, grade >35%, pause and GPS gap. No wind measurement.

| Constant/formula | Value and source/rationale |
|---|---|
| Running grade polynomial | `Cr(i)=155.4i⁵−30.4i⁴−43.3i³+46.3i²+19.5i+3.6` J/kg/m, Minetti et al. 2002 [J Appl Physiol](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/japplphysiol.01177.2001); divide by flat 3.6 to normalize to requested 1 kcal/kg/km. |
| Walking steep-descent polynomial | `Cw(i)=280.5i⁵−58.7i⁴−76.8i³+51.9i²+19.6i+2.5` J/kg/m, same Minetti paper; downhill cost scaled to the ACSM flat 0.5 kcal/kg/km via `/2.5`. |
| Walking flat/climb | `0.5+9i` kcal/kg/km, derived from [ACSM walking equation](https://www.depts.ttu.edu/ksm/_documents/grad/acsm_comps/6a_Formulas_for_Met_Calucations-Table_7.3.pdf) net 0.1 + 1.8 grade ml/kg/m, using 5 kcal/L; resting 3.5 ml/kg/min excluded. |
| Descending floors | Running 0.35, walking 0.3 kcal/kg/km: conservative product safeguards against negative/implausibly low costs on steep grades. |
| Grade/model domain | ±0.35 bounded conservatively within Minetti study's approximately ±0.45 observed gradient range; invalid/noisy altitude uses flat cost. |
| Rolling `Crr` road/MTB/trekking-city/electric | 0.005 / 0.012 / 0.008 / 0.010: **illustrative product presets** for smooth road vs knobby/utility tires, not measured individual tires; cycling mechanical-force structure follows [Martin et al. 1998](https://collections.lib.utah.edu/dl_files/b4/8e/b48ef26086091662c561e673d7bd990d77868437.pdf). |
| Aerodynamic `CdA` road/MTB/trekking-city/electric | 0.32 / 0.45 / 0.42 / 0.48 m²: **illustrative posture presets** (tucked road vs upright), not rider-measured; no wind data. |
| Bike masses road/MTB/trekking-city/electric | 9 / 14 / 15 / 24 kg: explicit representative product assumptions; rider mass is the entered weight, not guessed. Missing bike selects trekking-city preset (documented fallback); missing electric assist selects medium. |
| Cycling force and energy | `F=(rider+bike)*9.81*(Crr+grade)+0.5*1.225*CdA*v²`; `max(5 W,max(0,F*v)*humanShare)*seconds/(0.24*4184)` kcal; Martin's rolling/aero/gravity work balance, 9.81 m/s² standard gravity, 1.225 kg/m³ sea-level air density, 4184 J/kcal thermochemical conversion, 24% **assumed** gross efficiency per feature request. |
| Coasting | Negative mechanical demand clips to zero and 5 W mechanical-equivalent light baseline, product heuristic for near-zero movement; no regenerating calories. |
| Electric motor contributions low/medium/high | 30% / 50% / 70%, requested illustrative assist shares; remaining human portions 70% / 50% / 30% applied to positive mechanical demand only, baseline remains 5 W. |
| Sex and age factors | Female ×0.98, male/unspecified ×1; age ≤40/unspecified ×1, above 40 minus 0.1% per year capped at 4% (80+). **Small product heuristics, NOT validated individual corrections**, not claims supported by Minetti/ACSM. |
| GPS thresholds | ≥8m or endpoint-accuracy displacement, gap ≤30s, raw speed ≤45m/s: pre-existing accepted tour engine; rolling grade's ≥20m window, ≤2m/s vertical, ≤35% slope and altitude precisions are conservative noise rejection choices, not measured sensor guarantees. |

The hiking ACSM positive-grade calculation and walking Minetti descending branch meet at flat grade by construction, but this remains a coarse distance-based estimate, not individual exercise energy. Cycling's watts use GPS segment displacement/time rather than wheel-power measurement.
