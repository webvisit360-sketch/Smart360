## Preverjanje v brskalniku in PDF

- Posnetek dejanske razvojne strani `/admin/login`: `reports/smart360-login-cgp-header.jpg` (950 × 760 px). Brez prijave.
- Computed-style pregled dejanskega elementa `.smart-login__logo span`: `textContent=SMART360`, `font-family=Archivo, sans-serif`, `font-weight=800`, `font-size=26px`, `letter-spacing=0.52px` (= 0.02em), `color=rgb(18, 26, 20)` (= #121A14); ozadje nadrejenega logotipa `rgb(255, 255, 255)` (= #FFFFFF); `document.fonts.status=loaded` in `document.fonts.check('800 26px Archivo', 'SMART360')=true`. Znak ima izvor `/brand/smart360-znak-40.png`.
- Izoliran PDFKit izris istega novega bloka A6 besednega znaka v začasni `/tmp/smart360-wordmark-pdf-layout-qa.pdf`: format 297.638 × 419.528 pt (105 × 148 mm), `pdftotext` prebere **SMART360**, `pdffonts` potrdi vdelan **Archivo-ExtraBold**. Dejanskega avtenticiranega endpointa za A6 nalepko ni mogoče preskusiti brez avtorizacije; vzorčni PDF zato ni dokaz celotne nalepke.
- Namenski PDF QR-nalepke (`artifacts/api-server/src/lib/guideReadyNotice.ts`, `makeReadySticker`) je **nedotaknjen**; ne vsebuje znaka.
- `pnpm --filter @workspace/smart360 typecheck`, `pnpm --filter @workspace/api-server typecheck`, `pnpm --filter @workspace/mockup-sandbox typecheck` in `git diff --check` uspešni.

# Email lockup audit

Scope: existing email wordmarks only. `Smart360` in ordinary message copy, headings, subjects, footers and sender addresses is **not** a brand-wordmark element. The public image URL follows the existing `https://smart360.info/brand/…` pattern; this audit does not imply publication or email delivery.

Before the update, the shared `renderEmail` kicker in `emailTemplate.ts` displayed the official 20 × 20 mark (`smart360-znak-40.png`) followed by HTML brand text. Its text had `font-size:13px`, `font-weight:800`, `letter-spacing:.14em`, `text-transform:uppercase`, and `color:#121A14`; the welcome theme specified `Archivo,-apple-system,BlinkMacSystemFont,'Segoe UI',Roboto,Arial,sans-serif`, while the other theme used `-apple-system,'Segoe UI',Roboto,Arial,sans-serif`. The ready-notice override displayed the mark at 46 × 46 and HTML `SMART360` text in `Archivo,-apple-system,BlinkMacSystemFont,'Segoe UI',Roboto,Arial,sans-serif`, `font-size:13px`, `font-weight:800`, `letter-spacing:.14em`, and **green `#157347`** (no explicit `text-transform` on that uppercase literal). No HTML body copy or tenant name needs to be converted to an image.

After the update, *every existing brand-mark image* in these templates uses **one** opaque white RGB lockup: `artifacts/smart360/public/brand/smart360-email-lockup-558x138.png` (558 × 138 pixels). The official unchanged SVG mark and actual loaded `Archivo-800.ttf` render the embedded uppercase wordmark in `#121A14` at `.02em` spacing, with no HTML wordmark. The complete lockup is displayed at 81 × 20 in shared headers or 186 × 46 in manual ready notices, with both HTML dimensions and matching inline CSS. The largest display has 3× raster resolution. The SVG and actual font are drawn together on white at 4× the final resolution and the composite is Lanczos3-downsampled.
